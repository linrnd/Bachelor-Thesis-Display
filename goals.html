Goals

The expected output of the project is a high-quality visualization, which should fulfill the following criteria:

• Produce a very wide images, with a resolution of 12288 x 1200 pixels. This is a 10.24:1 aspect ratio, which is suitable for the 360-degree screen .

• The visualization must be functional, serving the purpose of storytelling while remaining clear, informative, and uncluttered, it should enable viewers to discover patterns or connections that are not explicitly visible in the raw datasets. The greatest value of a picture is when it forces us to notice what we never expected to see. In addition, the functionality of a graph can also contribute to its aesthetic appeal.

• The output should be sightful, going beyond a basic graph representation to convey meaning and even emotions in a way that makes it memorable to the viewer.

• Besides supporting practical concerns, the final result must also be aesthetically pleasing.

• This project aims to explore and evaluate scripting and visualization tools, such as Cytoscape , by testing them within a real-world project. The objective is to compare their functionalities and derive a conclusion on how they can be effectively combined in practice for graph drawing.

• This project explores how graph drawing and network visualization can be used to support and promote research in the applied field of social science, as mentioned in the introduction. It investigates how graph drawing techniques can be employed to analyze and interact with relational datasets more effectively and creatively.
